How the AI Works

From raw multimedia uploads to personalized trivia questions and psychometric duo evaluation.

01

Multimodal Ingestion & OCR

The vision-language model parses unformatted screenshots, camera roll photos, and raw chat logs. Key entities, timestamps, and recurring inside jokes are extracted into semantic nodes.

02

Lore Vectorization & Distractor Generation

Memories are clustered into contextual topics (travel lore, late-night quotes, cafeteria mishaps). Plausible wrong answers are synthesized from real conversation context to test genuine recall.

03

Real-Time Minigame Synthesis

Questions are dynamically compiled based on the active duo pair, balancing photo identification with chat trivia and inside joke references.

04

Psychometric Friendship Scoring

Evaluates answer accuracy, response latency, and streak momentum to calculate an aggregate friendship compatibility score (0–100%) with qualitative diagnostics.
